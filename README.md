HUNTER: A rugged survivalist who has meticulously studied the habits of the most ferocious creatures in the wild. He can stalk prey for days in the densest wilderness, waiting for the perfect moment for decisive attack that brings another trophy for his collection.

Benefits:  
• +3 bonus to attack and damage rolls against animals (including spiders) and lycanthropes;  
• Can achieve proficiency (3 slots) in any ranged weapon skill;  
• At 5th level, gains immunity to web, grease, and entanglement;  
• At 7th level, gains a +2 bonus to AC and saving throws against animals and lycanthropes;  
• At 9th level, gains the Decisive Attack special ability, which can be used once per day; the Hunter gains an additional use of this attack at 13th and 17th levels.

DECISIVE ATTACK: Seizing the right moment, the Hunter strikes at a vital point. With this ability, each attack in the next round deals a critical hit.

Disadvantages:  
• cannot wear armor heavier than hide;  
• cannot use swords, halberds, flails, hammers, or spiked maces;  
• cannot learn the Sword and Shield style.

***  
  
Reasonable Ranger additions:  
• Alertness: Rangers gain a +2 sight radius  
• Toughness: Rangers gain a +4 bonus to death saving throws and a +25% bonus to poison resistance
